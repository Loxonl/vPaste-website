## Generation prompts

### local-history

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium editorial illustration for a local-first clipboard application website, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: communicate local storage, orderly reusable records and moving an archive, through a beautifully photographed physical still life, not an infographic.
Scene: seamless pale mist-blue studio surface and backdrop matching #edf3f5. A small, elegantly machined brushed-aluminium desktop archive box with its shallow drawer gently open, holding a tidy set of thin ivory and slate-blue index cards. A slim unbranded silver portable solid-state drive lies next to it; two blank cards sit neatly apart as if being sorted. The objects form one coherent quiet composition, not separate tiles.
Style: photorealistic high-end industrial-design editorial photography, real paper fibres and satin aluminium, soft daylight from upper left, gentle contact shadows, restrained palette, fine detail, no plastic toy look.
Composition: wide, low three-quarter perspective, main objects centred with generous negative space, everything fits with open margins. Background fills canvas, no border.
Constraints: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, screen UI, padlocks, shields, cloud symbols, arrows, floating elements, gradients-as-decoration, sparkles or watermark. No claim of encryption. Make it elegant and tangible, not a generic 3D SaaS illustration.

### desktop-windows

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original desktop wallpaper for a Windows preview inside a clipboard app website. Landscape 16:10 composition, edge-to-edge wallpaper only, not a laptop or monitor picture.
Primary request: a beautifully lit physical paper sculpture, two broad softly folded sheets of matte ivory and dusty slate-blue paper crossing diagonally in a quiet architectural composition. Macro editorial photography with fine paper texture and subtle grounded shadows, almost abstract but convincingly physical. Mist-blue, chalk white, muted steel-blue; harmonise with a #edf3f5 website. Natural diffuse daylight, elegant understated desktop wallpaper.
Composition: interesting folds in the upper and right portions, open and low contrast in the lower third so a real application window can be overlaid. Broad, spacious shapes, no small clutter.
Constraints: original artwork, NOT the Windows bloom logo or official wallpaper; no text, icons, logos, taskbar, app window, desktop UI, borders, screen hardware, neon, glossy plastic blobs, stock infographic or watermark.

### desktop-macos

Primary request: tranquil coastal headland at first light, pale limestone dunes descending into a quiet slate-blue sea, distant coastline disappearing softly into cool morning mist. Fine natural texture, premium large-format landscape photography, subtle warm ivory sand against desaturated blue-grey water and pale blue sky. Harmonise with a mist-blue #edf3f5 website without making the whole scene monochrome.
Composition: restrained sweeping coastline from lower left towards upper right, low horizon, generous calm negative space, lower third low contrast for an application overlay.
Constraints: no people, buildings, boats, text, icons, logos, dock, desktop UI, screens, device hardware, giant sun, saturated sunset, fantasy mountains, HDR glow or watermark. Original landscape rather than a reproduction of an official Apple wallpaper. Quiet, elegant and natural.
